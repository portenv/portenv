# Milestone 1.2b: split view, first version

Date: 2026-10-18

## What exists now

- **Up to four panes in one window** (PLAN 4b). Each pane is its own `MainTabTerminal` streaming one of the workspace's tabs through the helper (ADR 0020), with its own Reconnecting and Disconnected. A tab is in at most one pane, so two panes never fight over a current tab, and an agent typing in a tab never moves a pane.
- **Split Right (⌘D) and Split Down (⇧⌘D)**, as title-bar buttons and in the Window menu. A new pane shows the next tab the tab bar shows that no pane shows; when every tab is in a pane, it makes a new shell tab, owned by you.
- **⌘⌥ and the arrows** move the focus between panes. **⌘W** closes the focused pane first and closes tabs only when one pane is left. Closing a pane never stops its tab.
- **The pane header**: the tab picker (the tabs no other pane shows), the running program, and a close button. The focused pane has a 1 px accent outline and takes the keys. VoiceOver reads each pane as "Pane: grok tab".
- **The tab bar** follows the focused pane: choosing a tab shows it in the focused pane, or focuses the pane already showing it. A small glyph marks the tabs that are in a pane and where.
- **The Team sidebar** filters the tab bar as before; a new pane takes the selected agent's next tab.

## Try it

1. Open a workspace (`make app`, then open Portenv). Make sure it has at least three tabs (⌘T).
2. Press ⌘D: the window splits side by side, and the right pane shows the next tab. Press ⇧⌘D: the right pane splits top and bottom. Or use the two buttons at the right of the title bar.
3. Press ⌘⌥← and ⌘⌥→, ⌘⌥↑ and ⌘⌥↓: the accent outline moves, and what you type goes to the outlined pane. Clicking in a pane focuses it too.
4. Click a tab in the tab bar: it shows in the outlined pane, or, if another pane shows it, that pane gets the outline. The tab bar's glyph shows which tabs are in a pane.
5. Click a pane header's tab name: the menu lists the tabs no other pane shows. Choose one.
6. Run `top` in one pane, then close that pane with its × (or ⌘W). Show the tab again from the tab bar: `top` is still running.
7. Press ⌘W until one pane is left; the next ⌘W closes the tab, as before.
8. With an agent team, select an agent in the sidebar: the tab bar shows its tabs, and ⌘D opens its next tab in a new pane.

## Not in this version

The header's status beyond the running program (portenvd doesn't send it yet), remembering the layout per workspace, drag to rearrange, "Opened by <agent>" headers, and the Warp-style features beyond what each terminal already does. See PLAN 4b.
